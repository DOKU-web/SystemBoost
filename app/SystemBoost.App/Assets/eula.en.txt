## Article 1 (Subject of this agreement)
This agreement applies between the Seller and the person who installs or uses SystemBoost (the app, engine, updates and documents, the "Program"; that person, the "User"). The User agrees to this agreement on the app's consent screen before using the Program.
In this agreement, the "sales page" means the app's ★ Plans screen, the app's Support, and the help page (/help) of the Seller's server.

## Article 2 (License)
1. The Seller gives the User a non-exclusive license to use the Program on the User's own computer. A User who bought bundle keys may also use it on computers that User manages (such as a company's work computers or family computers).
2. The Program is licensed, not sold. This agreement transfers no rights in the Program or its components to the User.
3. The User may not transfer the license to anyone else.

## Article 3 (Plans and license keys)
1. The Free plan can be used without a key.
2. The Pro, Ultra and Business plans are opened with a license key issued by the Seller. The features of each plan, and what each feature needs to work, are as shown on the app's ★ Plans screen at the time of purchase.
3. With a monthly or yearly key, paid features lock when the stated period ends and the app returns to the Free plan. A key bought by direct payment, such as a bank transfer, is not charged automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. A key bought as a card subscription is charged the amount shown at purchase automatically every period (monthly or yearly) until the User cancels, and is extended by one period with each payment. The User can cancel at any time through the subscription management link in the payment email (the app's Support also shows the way). Cancelling stops the next automatic payment; the period already paid can be used until it ends, and then the app returns to the Free plan. This is the same when a yearly subscription is cancelled during its period. Money for a period already paid is refunded on withdrawal under Article 9, in the case of Paragraph 10 of this Article, and wherever else the law provides for a refund. If a payment does not go through, the key is not extended.
4. A lifetime key has no limit on its period of use. This does not mean it will work on every future operating system and game.
! 5. A key is for the buyer's own use only. It may not be given to others or made public. Bundle keys follow Paragraph 9.
6. If a key is made public or misused, the Seller may block it. A blocked key returns to the Free plan when the app starts online.
7. A key received with a referral code or an invite code can be used only for the period shown. The Seller may end a referral code at any time. A key already received stays usable for its period.
8. Members can create invite codes. Invite rewards are received as Pro keys for the period shown in the app. A member who uses an invite code at sign-up receives the discount ticket shown in the app. A ticket can be used once on a direct payment within the period shown, and cannot be exchanged for cash or given to anyone else. The Seller may cancel rewards obtained by improper means, such as false accounts, and block those accounts.
! 9. Bundle keys are several yearly keys of the same plan bought at once. One key is used on one computer. The buyer may give the people who use the computers the buyer manages (such as employees or family) the key for that computer, and may not sell, give or make keys public to anyone else. The Seller blocks only a key that was made public or misused; the other keys of the same bundle stay usable. Bundle keys are not charged automatically and cannot take a discount ticket.
10. If the Seller rejects a direct-payment order, such as a bank transfer, instead of approving it, no key is issued. Any money already received for that order is returned by the Seller without delay, at the latest within 3 business days of receiving it, or the Seller takes the steps needed to return it within that time.
11. If a minor buys a paid plan without the consent of a legal representative, the minor or the legal representative may cancel that purchase. This does not apply where the Civil Act does not allow cancellation, for example when the purchase was made with property the legal representative allowed the minor to use.

## Article 4 (Prohibited acts)
The User may not:
! 1. Bypass or remove the license check or plan locks, or distribute a means of doing so
! 2. Copy, sell, rent or redistribute the Program or keys
3. Modify or reverse engineer the Program, except where the law or the license of an open source component allows it
4. Use the published code without permission
5. Remove copyright notices from the Program

## Article 5 (Games and anti-cheat)
1. The Program captures the game's picture and shows it in a separate overlay. It does not inject code into game processes and does not change game memory or game files.
! 2. The Seller has not tested the Program in games that use anti-cheat. The Seller does not guarantee that the Program complies with the terms of use or anti-cheat policy of every game.
! 3. The User must check a game's policy before using the Program with it.

## Article 6 (Features that change system settings)
1. Game boost, the work and streaming features (stream mode, work mode, startup programs), overclocking and power limits run only when the User turns them on. The Program backs up the previous values and provides a way to restore them.
! 2. Overclocking can cause game crashes, system freezes and driver errors. It may also affect the graphics card manufacturer's warranty.
Use small values first, after understanding the risks.
! 3. The Seller does not guarantee that automatic recovery always works when a driver or hardware error has occurred.

## Article 7 (Performance)
1. Frame generation does not increase the frames the game actually draws. It inserts images between frames.
! 2. Turning on frame generation increases input delay. It is not recommended for competitive games.
3. Output FPS follows the monitor's refresh rate unless the User chooses an output rate. A chosen rate can exceed the refresh rate, but a monitor displays only up to its own refresh rate.
4. Measurements on the sales page are results in the stated environment. Actual results differ by PC, game and settings.

## Article 8 (Network use and personal information)
1. The Program sends the key's identifying number to the Seller's license server to check the key. It sends the subscription number and the key in use when renewing a subscription key, and the order code and the payer name and payment memo the User entered when paying directly. It connects to GitHub to check for updates. Setting up the Windows game environment on macOS downloads the files it needs.
The Seller's server keeps the full text of each key it issues and the key's record (plan, issue and expiry dates, email address, subscription number, note). The email address is the payment email of a card payment or an email the Seller entered, and it is also inside the key. If the Seller approves a direct payment with the note left empty, the payer name and payment memo go into the key record's note. No retention period is set for key records or for the index that finds keys by email address; they are kept until the Seller deletes them.
Orders keep the order code, plan, payment method, the payer name and payment memo of a direct payment, and the email address, and are deleted 90 days after the order last changed. The Seller's activity log also keeps email addresses and notes for 180 days.
The Seller's server and its data are processed and stored in data centers that Cloudflare, Inc. (a US company) runs around the world, so personal information is transferred abroad. Member information is stored in Supabase's database service; its location is shown in the privacy policy.
2. While it is on, the Program sends the Seller's server a randomly created install number, the app version, operating system, plan, frame generation multiplier, and whether one-click game boost is in use, for usage statistics. The server also records the country of the connection and the times of the first and last connection. Keys, email addresses, game names and hardware information are not sent, and usage statistics store no IP address. The install number is deleted 90 days after the last connection.
3. Usage statistics are optional. The User chooses whether to send them on the screen where the User agrees to this agreement, and can change this later in the app's settings. When they are off, the Program sends no usage statistics.
4. When the User enters a referral code, the Program sends the code and a randomly created device number to the Seller's server. Signing up as a member also sends the device number, but the server keeps it only when an invite code is used. The device number is used only to stop the same device from using a code again, and is deleted after 90 days.
5. Signing up as a member is optional and open only to people aged 14 or over. For members, the Seller keeps the ID, the password and recovery code as hash values, the times of sign-up and last login, the version and time of the notice agreed to, the invite code, the inviting member, the number of invitations and reward days, discount tickets (including the order code a ticket was used on), failed logins and lockouts, whether the member is blocked, and login sessions in Supabase's database service. This information is used to manage members, invite rewards and discount tickets. If a member sends a request to the support center while signed in to the app, the member's ID is attached to it.
When a member leaves, the member information in the live database is deleted at once, and a copy stays only in the backups described in Paragraph 9. The ID remains, however, in the record of an invite reward key (until the Seller deletes it), the Seller's activity log (180 days) and support requests (for their retention period).
6. To prevent abuse of sign-up, login and the support center, the Seller keeps hash values made from the connecting IP address for a short time. The hash values are made with a value only the server knows. Those for sign-up and login are deleted at the hourly cleanup after the clock hour they were counted in ends; those for the support center stay on the Seller's server for up to 1 hour after the last request. A connection that enters a wrong password on the Seller's dashboard is also counted by a hash value instead of the IP address, kept for 15 minutes after the last wrong attempt.
7. When the User sends a request to the support center, the Seller keeps its category, title, text, the Seller's replies, the time of each message, its status, the time of consent, the language and a hash value of the access code. If the User enters or chooses them, the Seller also keeps the contact, order code, the key's identifying number and app information (app version, operating system, GPU name). A refund request also carries its reason and the order and key information the server has (plan, the dates the key was received and expires, how it was received, subscription number, order code, payer name, order status, payment method). A full key sent for a refund request or subscription management is used only for checking and is not stored. Requests about payments and refunds are kept for 3 years and other requests for 1 year, counted from the day the request last changed.
8. The privacy policy can be seen in the app's settings screen, in the support center, and on the /privacy page of the Seller's server.
9. Deleted records stay for a short time in the backup and recovery features of the service companies. Records kept in Cloudflare's database (Durable Objects), such as usage statistics, stay for up to 30 days after deletion, and member information kept in Supabase for up to 7 days after deletion, and are then gone. The Seller uses these copies only to recover from a failure, and after a recovery deletes again the records that had been deleted before.

## Article 9 (Purchase and withdrawal)
1. The User may withdraw from the purchase within 7 days of receiving the key.
! 2. Once the User registers a key and starts using paid features, the digital content is treated as provided and withdrawal may be limited.
The Seller offers a free plan that can be used before purchase. Features found only in paid plans cannot be tried before purchase.
3. If the Program differs from what was displayed or advertised, or is provided differently from the contract, the User may withdraw within 3 months of receiving it, or within 30 days of the day the User learned or could have learned of this.
4. When a refund is complete, the Seller blocks the key.
5. Questions about withdrawal and refunds go to the support center in the app or on the help page, or to the contact shown on the sales page.

2. The Seller compensates for damage caused by the Seller's intent or gross negligence.
! 3. For damage caused by the Seller's minor negligence, compensation covers ordinary damage, up to the amount the User paid for the Program or 100,000 won, whichever is greater. For a User who uses only the Free plan, the limit is 100,000 won.
4. The Seller is not liable for compatibility problems caused by changes outside the Seller's control, such as operating system, driver and game updates, except where the Seller acted with intent or gross negligence.

! 2. If the User breaches Article 3, Paragraph 5 or 9, or Article 4, the Seller may block the key concerned and end the license.
